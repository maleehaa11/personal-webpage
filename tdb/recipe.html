Spicy Honey Garlic Chicken Bites

A perfect balance between sweet, spicy and savory!

Prep Time:

10 minutes


Cook Time:

15 minutes


Servings:

2


Ingredients:

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp red chili flakes (optional)
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds (for garnish)
  • 1 green onion (for garnish)

Instructions:

  1. Coat the Chicken: In a bowl, toss the chicken pieces with cornstarch, salt, and black pepper until evenly coated. This helps create a crispy texture when fried.
  2. Cook the Chicken: Heat oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook for about 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and fully cooked. Remove and set aside.
  3. Make the Sauce: In the same pan, melt the butter. Add minced garlic and saute for 30 seconds until fragrant. Stir in honey, soy sauce, hot sauce, and red chili flakes. Cook for another minute until the sauce thickens slightly.
  4. Coat the Chicken: Return the cooked chicken to the pan and toss well in the sauce until fully coated.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Sprinkle sesame seeds and chopped green onions on top. Serve hot with rice, noodles, or as a snack!
